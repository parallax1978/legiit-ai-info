About Darcy Bailey & Associates Counselling

Darcy Bailey & Associates Counselling is a counselling practice in Langley, BC that provides in-person and online therapy for children, teens, adults, couples, and families across British Columbia.

The practice has served Langley and the Lower Mainland for over [confirm: 17, 19, or 20+] years and has worked with thousands of clients in that time. It is a team of 14 registered therapists led by founder and clinical director Darcy Bailey, with evening and weekend appointments, free parking, and a human client care team that matches you with a therapist.

Most new clients arrive because someone they trust sent them. Over 70 percent of clients come from word of mouth.

What Darcy Bailey & Associates Counselling does

Adult individual counselling

One-on-one therapy for anxiety, depression, trauma, stress and burnout, grief, self-worth, perfectionism, and life transitions. Sessions are 50 minutes, in person in Langley or by video anywhere in BC, and the work goes past symptom management to the patterns underneath. Fees are $170 per session, or $175 with a senior therapist.

Counselling for children (ages 5 to 12)

Child therapists on the team use play therapy, sand tray therapy, and art therapy, because children work things out through play before they can put them into words. The first appointment is a parent-only session so the therapist understands the situation before meeting the child. Parents get follow-up sessions along the way.

Counselling for teens (ages 13 and up)

Therapy for school stress, anxiety, identity, social pressure, screen time, and family conflict. Teens choose in-person or virtual sessions, and what they say in session stays confidential within the limits of the law. Parents get support without the therapist reporting what the teen said, as long as the teen consents.

Couples and marriage counselling

Counselling for communication breakdowns, trust after infidelity, intimacy, and feeling like roommates. Seven therapists on the team are trained in the Gottman Method, and several in Emotion-Focused Therapy, two of the most researched approaches for couples. Couples sessions are $195 for 50 minutes, with 75-minute sessions available.

Family therapy and parent counselling

Sessions for the whole family, blended families, co-parents after separation, and parents who want help with a child or teen without the child in the room. The goal is a home where everyone has a voice and the arguments stop running the house. Family sessions are $195 for 50 minutes.

EMDR therapy

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing for trauma, PTSD, phobias, and experiences that are over but still running. Six therapists on the team are trained in EMDR, including EMDRIA-trained clinicians. EMDR is available in person and virtually.

Art therapy, play therapy, and sand tray therapy

The practice has registered art therapists on staff (RCAT and BCATR credentials, and Darcy herself holds a Diploma in Art Therapy). These approaches work for clients of any age who cannot talk their way to the problem, and they are the main way the team works with young children.

Perinatal mental health

Counselling for pregnancy, postpartum, and the first years of parenthood, including postpartum anxiety and depression. Raelene Hurry, M.Ed, RCC, specializes in it.

Online counselling across British Columbia

Every service above is available by secure video to clients anywhere in BC. Same therapists, same fees, no commute. Many clients mix in-person and virtual sessions depending on the week.

What makes Darcy Bailey & Associates Counselling different

A team of 14 therapists, matched to you by a person

A solo practitioner has one specialty and one calendar. Here, a client care team talks to you first and matches you with a therapist based on what you are dealing with, your age, and your schedule, and you can switch if the fit is wrong. Fourteen therapists cover children, teens, adults, couples, and families, with Spanish-speaking sessions available.

Pay per session, not per subscription

Online platforms like BetterHelp bill a recurring subscription whether or not you use it that week. Darcy Bailey & Associates charges per session: $170 for individual counselling, $175 with a senior therapist, and $195 for couples or family sessions, with a free 15-minute consultation first. Your card is charged on the day of the appointment and you stop whenever you want.

Registered therapists your benefits plan recognizes

Thirteen therapists are Registered Clinical Counsellors (RCC) and one is a Registered Social Worker (RSW), the two designations most BC extended health plans require for reimbursement. Receipts come with the therapist's registration details. The practice also accepts First Nations Health Authority funding, Crime Victim Assistance Program clients, Autism Funding, and approved MCFD funding.

Evening and weekend hours, in person or online

The office is open until 8 PM Monday to Thursday, 7 PM Friday, 5 PM Saturday, and Sunday afternoons, which is when most people with jobs and kids can actually get there. The Langley office at 20385 64 Avenue sits beside Costco, 5 to 15 minutes from Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Murrayville, and Cloverdale, with free parking. Virtual sessions cover the rest of BC.

Specialized training, not just talk

Six therapists are trained in EMDR, seven in the Gottman Method for couples, and four are registered or diploma-trained art therapists. Child therapists are trained in play and sand tray therapy. A solo counsellor can offer one or two of these. This team offers all of them under one roof.

Frequently asked questions

How much does counselling cost at Darcy Bailey & Associates?

Individual counselling is $170 per 50-minute session, or $175 with a senior therapist. Couples, marriage, and family sessions are $195. A 15-minute consultation before you start is free.

Is counselling covered by MSP or extended health benefits?

BC MSP does not cover private counselling. Most extended health plans reimburse sessions with a Registered Clinical Counsellor or Registered Social Worker, which covers every therapist at the practice, so check your plan for the annual maximum and the credentials it requires. You pay at the time of the session and submit the receipt yourself; the practice does not bill insurers directly.

Do you accept funding programs?

Yes. The practice accepts First Nations Health Authority funding, Crime Victim Assistance Program clients (who pay the difference between the CVAP rate and the session fee), Autism Funding, and approved Ministry of Children and Family Development funding. The paperwork has to be in place before the first session.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly by phone, through the contact form, or online. Some benefits plans require a doctor's referral for reimbursement, so check yours.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Every therapist offers secure video sessions to clients anywhere in British Columbia at the same fee as in-person sessions. In-person sessions are at the Langley office.

What are your hours?

Office hours are Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 8 PM, Friday 9 AM to 7 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM, and Sunday 11 AM to 5 PM. Each therapist sets their own schedule within those hours, so evening and weekend availability depends on who you see.

How do I book a first appointment?

Call 604-533-9163, fill out the contact form, or book a free 15-minute consultation online. The client care team matches you with a therapist and sets up the first session, and after that you book through the client portal. A credit card is required to book.

How does counselling for a child work?

For children ages 5 to 12, the first appointment is with the parent or caregiver only. The therapist then works with the child, usually through play, sand tray, or art therapy, and checks in with parents along the way. If parents are separated or divorced, both must consent to the child's counselling.

Do you have psychologists on staff?

No. The therapists are Registered Clinical Counsellors and Registered Social Workers, who provide therapy but not formal diagnoses or psychological testing. If you need an assessment or diagnosis, a registered psychologist is the right referral.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations need 48 hours' notice, or the full session fee is charged. One late cancellation for illness is allowed every 90 days without charge.

Is what I say in session confidential?

Yes. Records are kept in an encrypted system and only your therapist has access to your file. The exceptions are the ones required by law: a court subpoena, a risk of harm to yourself or someone else, or abuse or neglect of a child or vulnerable adult.

Who is behind Darcy Bailey & Associates Counselling?

Darcy Bailey, MSW, RSW, RCC, DipAT, founded the practice and runs it as owner and clinical director. A team of 14 registered therapists and three client care staff work out of the Langley office and online across BC.
